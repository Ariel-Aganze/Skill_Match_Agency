Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our language programs? Here are some of the most common inquiries from our students. If you need further clarification, feel free to contact us directly.

What languages do you offer?

We offer a wide range of languages including Spanish, French, Mandarin, German, Italian, and Japanese. We are continuously expanding our offerings to meet the diverse needs of our students.

How long does it take to become fluent?

The time it takes to become fluent varies depending on the individual's dedication, the language's difficulty, and the intensity of the program. Our personalized plans are designed to accelerate your learning process.

What is your teaching methodology?

Our teaching methodology is a blend of communicative language teaching, task-based learning, and cultural immersion. We focus on practical communication skills to ensure you can use the language in real-world situations.
